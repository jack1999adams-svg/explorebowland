_Last updated: 12 July 2026_

This privacy policy explains how **Explore Bowland** ("we", "us", "our")
collects, uses and protects information when you visit
**www.explorebowland.co.uk** (the "site"). We are committed to handling your
data responsibly and in line with the UK GDPR and the Privacy and Electronic
Communications Regulations (PECR).

## Information we collect

- **Information you give us.** If you contact us (for example by email via our
  [Get in touch](/get-in-touch/) page), we receive the details you choose to
  send, such as your name, email address and message.
- **Information collected automatically.** Like most websites, our hosting
  provider records standard technical data such as your IP address, browser
  type, device information and the pages you visit, for security and to keep
  the site running.
- **Cookies and similar technologies.** See the section below.

## Cookies

A cookie is a small text file stored on your device. We use:

- **Essential cookies** — needed for the site to function and to remember your
  privacy choices. These do not require consent.
- **Advertising and measurement cookies** — set by our advertising partner
  (see below). These are only used where you have given consent.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings. Visitors in
the UK and the EEA are asked for consent via our consent banner before any
non-essential cookies are set, and can change or withdraw that consent at any
time.

## Advertising — Google AdSense

We use **Google AdSense**, a third-party advertising service provided by
Google, to display adverts on the site.

- Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your
  prior visits to this and other websites.
- Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads
  to you based on your visits to our site and/or other sites on the internet.
- For visitors in the UK, EEA and Switzerland, we use a Google-certified
  consent management platform to obtain consent before personalised advertising
  cookies are used, consistent with Google Consent Mode.

You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting
[Google Ads Settings](https://adssettings.google.com), and learn more from
[How Google uses information from sites that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). To opt
out of third-party vendors' use of cookies for personalised advertising, visit
[www.aboutads.info](https://www.aboutads.info/choices/).

## How we use your information

We use the information described above to:

- respond to your enquiries;
- operate, maintain and secure the site;
- display advertising that helps fund the site; and
- understand, in aggregate, how the site is used.

We do **not** sell your personal information.

## Legal bases (UK GDPR)

We rely on your **consent** for non-essential cookies and advertising; our
**legitimate interests** in running a secure, functioning website; and, where
you contact us, the steps necessary to **respond to your request**.

## Your rights

Under the UK GDPR you have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the
processing of your personal data, to object to processing, and to data
portability. You also have the right to withdraw consent at any time and to
lodge a complaint with the [Information Commissioner's Office (ICO)](https://ico.org.uk).
To exercise any of these rights, please [get in touch](/get-in-touch/).

## Data retention

We keep personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described
above, or as required by law. Enquiry emails are kept only as long as needed to
handle your request.

## Third-party links

The site contains links to other websites. We are not responsible for the
privacy practices or content of those sites. Please review their own privacy
policies.

## Children

The site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect
personal data from them.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this
page with a revised "last updated" date.

## Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or how we handle your data,
please [get in touch](/get-in-touch/).
