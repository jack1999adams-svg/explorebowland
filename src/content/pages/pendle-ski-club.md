## Pendle Ski Club

---

Pendle Ski Club Set in a fantastic location on Pendle Hill.  Known as 'Nick of Pendle', the road takes you right across the western slopes of Pendle Hill and offers fantastic views of the surrounding countryside.

Next to the ski slope is The Wellsprings restaurant and bar, which is a lovely tapas bar, open all year round.  There area is excellent for walking, and offers an easier route up to the top of Pendle Hill than from Barley.  We've done this walk many times, <a href="https://www.explorebowland.co.uk/pendle-hill-from-nick-o-pendle/">here</a> is an example.

The club offers lessons for all levels and operates an annual membership fee.  It is a non-profit club, with all fees being reinvested into the club;
