<h2 style="text-align: center;">Forest of Bowland Wild Camping</h2>

<h3>Is wild camping permitted in the Forest of Bowland?</h3>
Generally the answer is no, but there is a certain area of Bowland which is sometimes open for wild campers.  There is a public group on Facebook where information can be obtained, although at the moment the site is closed do to Covid-19.

<a href="https://www.facebook.com/groups/313068975787403?ref=share" target="_blank" rel="noopener">Facebook link</a>.

There is also a spot available just outside the Forest of Bowland, with excellent views of Pendle Hill.  Details below.
