# A stroll along Langden Valley

---

On a cold, crisp but stunningly clear day, Tim, Waj and Dave head up the beautiful Langden Valley to catch the autumn sun amidst the gorgeous twisty Langden Brook.

### Trough of Bowland

Langden Valley sits along the Trough of Bowland (or Trough Road) which is one of the most beautiful roads in the country (in our humble opinion of course).  The road is famously used by Bradley Wiggins for his training and you may see many eager cycling wannabees.  On a Sunday afternoon particularly The Trough is frequented by vintage  cars and lots of motorbikes.
