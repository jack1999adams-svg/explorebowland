# Elterwater

---

A sunny, chilly walk around the valleys of the Lakeland Fells.

Park near the local pub, Brittania Inn and make sure you finish off with a pint.  Hopefully the weather will be kind like it was for us today.

### Cathedral Cave

An amazing feature nearby to this walk (we forgot how close it was today so didn't visit) is Cathedral Cave or Quarry.

https://www.visitcumbria.com/amb/cathedral-cave/
