The Parkers Arms is a delightful little pub in the valley just beyond the Newton Fells - if you're heading from Clitheroe.  It can also be accessed from Dunsop Bridge to the west and Slaidburn on the eastern side of the Forest of Bowland.

The gastro pub offers locally sourced food and has won lots of awards over the years, including <a href="https://www.top50gastropubs.com/pub-profile/the-parkers-arms/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Top 50 Gastro Pub</a>.

If the restaurant is busy, why not sit outside in the fabulous beer garden.  The Parkers Arms is a real treat.
