# Ingleton Waterfalls

---

Just a couple of miles beyond the northern most tip of the Forest of Bowland lies the famous Ingleton Waterfalls Trail.

The trail offers a relatively easy 7km walk around some spectacular scenery with spectacular views of some roaring waterfalls (especially after rain).  Today, unfortunately, mist landed as I approached Pecca Falls and became so bad I ended up cutting the walk short.  This actually was still makes a worth while walk to post because for those who aren't quite up to full fitness or are pushed for time, it is a quicker way around the Ingleton Waterfalls although bear in mind you won't get to see all of it.

Also remember that Ingleton Waterfalls does charge.
