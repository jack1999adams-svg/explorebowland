Tim writes (16/07/2020): Park up at Nick o Pendle and walk towards The Wellsprings.  Come off the road to the left and head down the track towards Wymondhouses Farm.

Continue towards Cold Coats farm at the bottom of fell.  I seem to recall that I was met with a herd of cows here and at one point was partially charged at.

The track meets Pendleton Road briefly, and then take a left towards Parker Place Farm.  Here there is a bit of climb up the track through Wiswell Eaves Farm, approaching the wireless station.

There is a decent track back to Nick o Pendle along the bottom of the moor, passing Wilkin Heys and Parsley Barn.
