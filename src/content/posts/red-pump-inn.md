The Red Pump Inn is tucked away between Longridge Fell and Clitheroe in the Forest of Bowland AONB.

We last visited in August 2020 and stopped for two nights in one of the camping yurts.  The weather wasn't kind but we had the pleasure of eating breakfast and tea in the pub.  Despite the storm we also managed a short walk along the River Hodder <a href="http://www.explorebowland.co.uk/river-hodder-walk"><strong>here</strong></a>.

The food was delicious and a special mention must go out to the 50 day dry aged prime rib steak!

You can book your stay at the Red Pump Inn <strong><a href="https://www.booking.com/hotel/gb/the-red-pump-inn.en.html?aid=2066846&amp;no_rooms=1&amp;group_adults=2">here</a></strong>.
