	<h2 class="wprm-fallback-recipe-name">Bowland Beef Meatballs</h2>
	<img class="wprm-fallback-recipe-image" src="https://www.explorebowland.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/Bowland-Beef-meatballs-80x80.jpg"/>	<p class="wprm-fallback-recipe-summary">
		<p>Delicious homemade meatballs with Forest of Bowland beef</p>	</p>
	<div class="wprm-fallback-recipe-equipment">
			</div>
	<div class="wprm-fallback-recipe-ingredients">
		<h4>For the meatballs</h4><ul><li>1 Pack Forest of Bowland Beef mince</li><li>1 Pack Fresh Parsley ((Dried will do))</li><li>1 Brioche Bun ((Or other breadcrumbs))</li><li>1 Salt &amp; Ground Black Pepper to taste</li></ul><h4>For the sauce</h4><ul><li>2 Tins Chopped tomatoes</li><li>1 Pack Quality plumb tomatoes ((Cherry tomatoes will do))</li><li>2 Medium Red Onions</li><li>1 Bulb Garlic ((We like a LOT of garlic))</li><li>1 Diced Courgette</li><li>1 Large glass Red wine</li><li>1 Cube Vegetable stock</li><li>1 Cube Chicken stock</li><li>Half Cube Beef stock</li><li>1 Bunch Fresh parsley (Chopped)</li><li>1 Tbsp Dried Oregano</li><li>1 Tsp Paprika</li><li>1 Salt &amp; Black Pepper</li></ul>	</div>
	<div class="wprm-fallback-recipe-instructions">
		<h4>The meatballs</h4><ol><li><p>Blitz the brioche bun in a blender to make breadcrumbs</p></li><li><p>Put the beef in a large bowl and add the breadcrumbs</p></li><li><p>Chop up some of parsley and add to the bowl, and then and salt and black pepper to taste</p></li><li><p>Mix well with your hands</p></li><li><p>Griddle the meatballs to brown them well and set aside</p></li></ol><h4>The sauce</h4><ol><li><p>Dice the red onions and gently cook them in quality virgin olive oil</p></li><li><p>Add the garlic, we blitz ours with a little water and save two pieces to thinly slice</p></li><li><p>Add the diced courgette</p></li><li><p>Add the red wine, tinned tomatoes and plumb tomatoes and cook on a medium heat</p></li><li><p>Add all of the stock cubes, and oregano (don&#039;t put the chopped parsley in yet)</p></li><li><p>Add the meatballs and leave the sauce to simmer gently</p></li><li><p>After about 90 minutes add the chopped parsley and check the seasoning</p></li><li><p>Serve with your pasta of choice, we used linguine</p></li><li><p>Add some freshly grate parmareggio if you like</p></li></ol>	</div>
